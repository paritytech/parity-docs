!!! note "Target environments"
    The Apps tooling selects its target environment through configuration, such as the `-n` preset for `cdm` (`-n paseo`, `-n devnet`) or `--env` for the dotNS CLI. The `playground` CLI targets Paseo Next v2. Each environment is deployed, maintained, and supported by its operator, so report a problem with a network or a service on it to that environment's operator. Report bugs in the software itself, such as the `playground` CLI or the Product SDK, in the tool's own GitHub repository. See [Choose the Right Channel](/get-support.md#choose-the-right-channel) for where to ask.

    - **Paseo Next v2** (the `paseo` preset, parachain 1500): The TestNet the Apps tooling and the `playground` CLI target by default. Its services include dotNS and the Playground directory. See [developers.polkadot.com](https://developers.polkadot.com/) for its current status and how to get TestNet funds.
    - **Products Devnet** (the `devnet` preset): Operated by the Polkadot Community Foundation (PCF), including its chains, registries, and deployments, using open-source technology developed by Parity. PCF also publishes its own builds of the Polkadot App, including the Google Play app, and of Polkadot Desktop, and the `pad` tooling that publishes to Browse. Report issues with these services in PCF's [`products-devnet-issues` repository](https://github.com/Polkadot-Community-Foundation/products-devnet-issues) or ask in `#devnet-discussion` on the [Polkadot Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/eD4jMYbC5U).
